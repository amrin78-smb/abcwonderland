# Parent Zone Specification (as implemented)

**Entry:** a small lock (top-right of Home). A quick tap only says "This part is for grown-ups." It needs a 2-second hold (ring fills), then the challenge "Tap <word>, then <word>" with 9 shuffled digit buttons. A wrong tap issues a new challenge. A back button closes the gate.

**Progress tab:** counts of letters Introduced / Practised / Showing recognition (of 26). Also shown: uppercase vs lowercase activity counts, tracing attempts and completions, words heard, matching / adventure / find counts, approximate play time, favourite modes (time spent, then starts), an A–Z grid coloured by state, the legend, a "not an assessment" disclaimer, and the four most recent activities.

**Settings tab:** voice, music and SFX volume (large sliders), Music on/off, Reduced motion, Simplified interaction, Learning profile (Explorer / Discoverer / Little Learner; resets adaptive levels), Adapt difficulty automatically, Gentle break reminder (Off / 10 / 20 / 30 min; shown only at natural pauses on Home), and a privacy statement.

**Reset tab:** an explanation of exactly what is erased (progress, history, stars, stickers) and what is kept (settings). "Reset progress…" → second explicit confirmation "Yes, erase progress" / "No, keep progress" → saved immediately.

Not yet implemented: language preference (only English exists), per-letter drill-down, export.
